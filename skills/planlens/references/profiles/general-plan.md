### General plan review

Review whether the proposal is coherent, necessary, executable, and verifiable.

Check:

- The affected user or owner is explicit; the current baseline, intended outcome, and acceptance signal are observable rather than a list of activities.
- Scope, non-goals, constraints, assumptions, and unknowns are explicit and consistent.
- Major decisions have a stated rationale and acknowledge meaningful alternatives or tradeoffs.
- Steps follow real dependencies and assign clear ownership where ownership matters.
- Risks have practical mitigations, stop conditions, or escalation paths.
- Validation and acceptance criteria demonstrate the intended outcome.
- Rollback or recovery is defined for consequential or difficult-to-reverse changes.
- Added layers, tools, handoffs, and process are justified by the outcome or a real constraint.

Report only issues that could materially change the decision or delivery. When information is missing, state the smallest information gap needed to judge the plan. A sound plan may have no material findings.
